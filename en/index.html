How it works

Lunch in 3 clicks

Providing lunch often seems like a hassle. With Sendwich it's simple: three steps, and everyone eats.

Step 1 of 3

Everything on one platform

The organisation keeps full control over the ordering process. The Sendwich management platform lets you easily track all orders, payments and deliveries.

  • Overview of all orders
  • Payments tracked automatically
  • All deliveries in one place

One bundled order

Each user makes their own choice in the app. All orders are then automatically bundled and sent to the supplier.

Hand out & enjoy

What is Sendwich?

Sendwich is a lunch ordering platform with an app. Employees, pupils and parents order and pay for their lunch individually in the app. All orders are automatically bundled and brought to the organisation in a single delivery.

Who is Sendwich for?

For companies, secondary schools and primary schools that want to offer lunch without admin work, and for suppliers such as sandwich bars, soup suppliers and hot-meal caterers.

How does ordering with Sendwich work?

Each user picks from the offer in the app. Sendwich collects all choices, bundles them automatically and passes the order on to the supplier.

How does payment work?

Everyone pays individually in the app. Companies receive one single invoice and schools no longer have to collect cash.

How is lunch handed out?

When the delivery arrives, you pull a distribution list from the system in one click, per class, department or location. Everyone quickly gets the right lunch. Prefer scanning? That is optional, with a unique QR code per order.

Where can I find the Sendwich app?

Sendwich is a web app: you simply open it in your browser at go.sendwich.be, on your phone, tablet or computer. There's nothing to install.
